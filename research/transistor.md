# Research Exercise: The Importance of Transistors and Logic Gates in Modern Computers

## 1. Introduction

Modern computers are extremely complex systems capable of performing billions or even trillions of operations per second. However, the foundation of these systems is built from relatively simple electronic components: **transistors**.

Transistors are combined to create **logic gates**, and logic gates are combined to create increasingly complex digital circuits such as adders, registers, arithmetic logic units (ALUs), memory, control units, and eventually complete processors.

This research exercise explores how these fundamental components form the foundation of modern computing.
