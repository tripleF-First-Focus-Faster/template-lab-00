    **5.Grace Hopper**
    >Grace Hopper was a major pioneer in programming languages. She helped develop** COBOL**, one of the earliest programming languages designed to make programming easier for businesses. She also helped popularize the** idea of using compilers** to translate human-readable instructions into machine code.

    **6.John Mauchly**
    >John Mauchly was one of the key people behind the development of ENIAC, one of the first large-scale electronic general-purpose computers. His work helped demonstrate that electronic computers could perform calculations much faster than mechanical systems.

    **7. J. Presper Eckert**
    >J. Presper Eckert worked with John Mauchly to design and build ENIAC. He played an important role in its electronic engineering and construction. Their work helped move computers from mechanical systems toward high-speed electronic machines.

    **8.Jack Kilby**
    >Jack Kilby helped make computers smaller by developing the integrated circuit (IC). An integrated circuit places multiple electronic components on a small piece of semiconductor material. This technology became essential for modern computers and electronic devices.

    **9.Robert Noyce**
    >Robert Noyce was another major pioneer of the integrated circuit. He developed a practical form of the IC using silicon technology. His work helped create the semiconductor industry that later produced increasingly powerful computer processors.
    **10.Gordon Moore**
    >Gordon Moore helped develop the semiconductor industry and was one of the founders of Intel. He became famous for Moore's Law, the observation that the number of transistors that could be placed on integrated circuits increased rapidly over time.
